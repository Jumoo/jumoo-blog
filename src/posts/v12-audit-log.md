New in uSync.Complete v12.2, the uSync Audit log allows you to track what changes are being made to your site from the various uSync tools. This includes Sync-Pack imports, pushing and pulling content and settings between sites, and creation and applying of restore points. 

![uSync Audit log dashboard](/images/2023/audit-log.png)

The audit log is available in the uSync tree in the settings section of your site. 

## Log Details. 

On the log dashboard you can click through any item to see details of what the change was.

![uSync Audit Details](/images/2023/audit-log-detail.png)

## Start Scheduled Jobs 
As well as viewing things that have happened you can also see details of things that are in progress and scheduled to happen in the future. 

If you want you can even choose to start a scheduled task, that might not yet be due from the dashboard. 

![uSync Audit Details](/images/2023/audit-log-scheduled.png)
