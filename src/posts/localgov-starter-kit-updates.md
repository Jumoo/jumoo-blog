Today we've rolled out a minor update to the [local gov starter kit](http://kevin.jumoo.co.uk/2014/localgov-starterkit-for-umbraco-7/ "Localgov Starterkit for Umbraco 7").

[![homepage_full](/images/2014/03/homepage_full-1024x637.png)](/images/2014/03/homepage_full.png)

Nothing major, just making it work better with the forthcoming Umbraco 7.1 release due out this week.

You can go and [download the package](http://our.umbraco.org/projects/starter-kits/localgov-starter-kit) to use on your local Umbraco installation from the package repository on the Umbraco community site.

Or, you can have a play around with our on-line demo at [umblocalgov.jumoo.co.uk](http://umblocalgov.jumoo.co.uk).
