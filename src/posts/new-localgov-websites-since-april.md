As part of the [LocalGov Page Speedy Index](http://blog.jumoo.co.uk/2014/the-council-speedy-indexish/ "The council speedy indexish") script each month, we capture a desktop and mobile screenshot of each local government website. They are not in a large resolution but they good enough to produce the [all council websites in one image](http://blog.jumoo.co.uk/2014/every-council-website-pictured/ "Every Council website, pictured"), and it means we have an archive of what each local government site looks like each month.

Over the last few months I have been aware of a couple of new website launches in local government, not least the new Edinburgh City Council site and the [new service and information sites from West Berkshire](http://philrumens.blogspot.co.uk/2014/05/a-tale-of-two-websites.html). So after the [latest run of the LocalGov page speedy](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1n-qZrahsCOICmMFCuKdeRtQ1S7IE1RRAVIi4UTvgOeU/edit?usp=sharing), I decided to go though the screenshots and see how many sites have changed since we started grabbing the screenshots on 1st April. I was expecting 5-6 launches but in fact there have been 15 local government website redesigns in the last 2 months, so here they all are.

It turns out there is a lot of change going on all the time.  Most of the sites are following the trend for more simplified, task-focused sites, although there a large proportion are still pushing big news sections upfront. Bearing in mind that news will count for less than 3% of traffic for most sites, the focus, there, is still on the organisation over the customer.
