Yesterday *(Sept 28th)* [Umbraco v9 was released](https://umbraco.com/blog/umbraco-9-release/) 🎉.

On the release day we posted updates updates to a number of our packages: 

- [BackOffice Themes](https://our.umbraco.com/packages/backoffice-extensions/backoffice-themes)
- [Linked Pages](https://our.umbraco.com/packages/backoffice-extensions/linked-pages/)
- [uSync 9](https://our.umbraco.com/packages/developer-tools/usync/)

Today (29th Sept) we are releasing the updates for our commercial packages:

- [Translation Manager](https://jumoo.co.uk/translate/)
- [uSync.Complete](https://jumoo.co.uk/usync/complete/)

uSync and Translation manager are big releases, and there are some changes to licencing - so its worth reading the separate posts for them.

# uSync
uSync 9 and uSync.Complete 9 are Umbraco 9 ready and even have backwards compatibility with v8 - so you can 
pick up v8 uSync files and import them into your Umbraco 9 site. 

We've updated the UI a little for uSync on Umbraco 9 , so its easier to see which bits you are updating and the add on packages (such as uSync.Forms and uSync.Vendr) will fit in nicely when added. 

![uSync dashboard](/images/2021/usyncdashboard.png)

 Read more about the [uSync & uSync.Complete release](/2021/usync-9-release/)

# Translation Manager
Translation Manager is all trendy and Multi-targeted now, meaning we will have a single release for both Umbraco v8 and Umbraco v9 (and beyond). 

This version includes all the same functionality as the v8 version and we will be working with out Translation partners to bring all of the translation connectors up to date for v9.

![Translation mgr 9](/images/2021/tm9.png)

Read more about the [Translation Manager 9 release](/2021/tm-9-release/)
