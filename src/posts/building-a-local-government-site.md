Last week we ([@conormoody](http://twitter.com/conormoody) and myself) spoke at another SOCITM conference about [top tasks ](http://www.gerrymcgovern.com/nt/2011/nt-2011-09-05-Top-task.htm)and the stuff we did on liverpool.gov.uk; it's been a really interesting couple of months, going around the country and talking to everyone and it's been good to get other people's reactions to what we've done as well as watching the evolving process online as more and more people think about it all.

One thing is for sure, we probably aren't going to give that presentation again, I'm finding it hard to believe that no one hasn't seen it, and really it's getting a bit boring
