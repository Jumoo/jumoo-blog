Companies selling "customer benefits", who actually mean benefits to the people they sold it to, not the people who use the service. <span style="color: #ff6600;">_They are not the customer, they are your client. Customers are people, clients are other businesses._</span>

When those customer benefits include ,“It helped us to realise our…” _<span style="color: #ff6600;">Measure benefits with real numbers; people were x happy, x more people succeeded, it was x cheaper, or x faster. If the measurements are soft and fluffy, then you aren't using numbers properly.</span>_

 ![It doesn](/images/2015/10/afghan.powerpoint.jpg) It doesn't have to be, and really shouldn't be this complex. 

When people map things onto very large wall-boards, with loads of points and crossing lines, just so they can show how complex something is. _<span style="color: #ff6600;">It probably isn't. Check you're not being bamboozled.</span>_
