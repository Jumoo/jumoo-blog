We've organised our first event this week - not that it's going to happen this week.

#UmbLiverpool is an umbraco meetup in liverpool, for people who use or want to use umbraco to talk about umbraco, and things like that.

The first of what will hopefully be regular meetups will be on the 22nd May @ DoES Liverpool, you can find out more on the [UmbLiverpool blog](http://umbliverpool.tumblr.com/) and[ sign-up to attend on the Event page](http://lanyrd.com/2013/umbliverpool/).

It's our first event so we are bound to have done some things wrong, but come anyway, it should be fun :)
