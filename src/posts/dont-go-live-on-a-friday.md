---
title: Don't go live on a Friday
tags:
  - agile

date: 2015-08-11 10:33:09
---

Unless you have mastered [continuous integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_integration), and are [delivering little and often](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/making-software/release-strategies.html), don't go live with big changes on a Friday.

When pushing big changes out there are two ways things can go wrong: catastrophically, or in some significantly nuanced way, that it takes a while to notice.

If something doesn't go to plan, you will either struggle with it all day, or you will notice a few hours later. Either way, on a Friday, your technical and support people will likely be depleted and thinking of the weekend, or have already disappeared when you need them.

Deploy big things on a Tuesday or Wednesday.
