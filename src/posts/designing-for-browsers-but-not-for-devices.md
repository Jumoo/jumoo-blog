It's fairly safe to assume that most people are designing their websites for the popular browsers ([IE](http://windows.microsoft.com/en-GB/internet-explorer/products/ie/home), [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/), [Firefox ](http://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/)and [Safari](www.apple.com/safari/)), however looking around, its still evident that people are not designing for mobile.

<address>The reality is more people probably visit your website on a mobile device than on all but one of these popular browsers (and yes you've guessed it that's Internet Explorer)</address>For [liverpool.gov.uk](http://liverpool.gov.uk) 21% of site traffic over the last month was from mobile devices, only 16% of traffic was from someone using Chrome.

The third most popular browser version on [liverpool.gov.uk](http://liverpool.gov.uk/) - after IE9 and IE8 is the Safari on IOS5.

while your working out if you need a mobile app or site, 1/5 of your potential site visitors are walking away - or worse phoning your call centre.
